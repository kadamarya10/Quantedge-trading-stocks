📐 Option Greeks Formulas & Buyer's Intelligence Guide
⚡ Black-Scholes-Merton Greek Equations (Heavily Used in NSE Option Premiums)
Δ Delta (Directional Speed) Calls: 0 to +1 | Puts: -1 to 0
Δ_CE = N(d₁)  |  Δ_PE = N(d₁) - 1
Measures how much the option price changes for a ₹1 move in NIFTY spot. At ATM, Delta is 0.50 (NIFTY moves 50 pts ➔ option gains ₹25).
Γ Gamma (Delta Acceleration) Maximum at ATM
Γ = N'(d₁) / [ S · σ · √T ]
The Option Buyer's Secret Weapon. Measures how fast Delta grows. When NIFTY rallies, Gamma pushes your Delta from 0.50 ➔ 0.70 ➔ 0.90, creating non-linear explosive compounding!
Θ Theta (Daily Time Decay) Buyer Decay / Day
Θ ≈ - [S · N'(d₁) · σ] / (2√T · 365)
The rent you pay each day for holding an option. Accelerates exponentially in the final 3 days before expiry, completely destroying cheap OTM options.
ν Vega (Volatility Sensitivity) ₹ per 1% IV shift
ν = [S · √T · N'(d₁)] / 100
Measures premium increase when India VIX rises by 1%. High Vega protects you if volatility explodes, but hurts during post-event IV crush.
🎯 Quant Guide: Which Option Strike Should You Buy?
1. The Sweet Spot (ATM / Delta 0.48 - 0.55):
Highest Gamma-to-Theta efficiency. You get the maximum explosive upside acceleration per rupee of decay paid. Ideal for intraday scalpers and momentum buyers.
2. Solid Trend (Slight ITM / Delta 0.55 - 0.70):
Moves strongly with spot price (₹0.60 - ₹0.70 per ₹1 move) with significantly lower percentage Theta decay. Best for directional swing trades.
3. The OTM Theta Trap (Delta < 0.30) — AVOID:
Cheap premium (e.g. ₹20-₹40) looks tempting, but has near-zero Gamma and 80%+ time decay. Even if NIFTY moves 30 points in your direction, Theta decay often causes net loss!
